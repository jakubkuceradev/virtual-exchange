A 24/7 trading competition. Fictional assets, real order-book behavior.

Now: API scaffold, /health, checks, and Docker/Compose. Trading modules below are planned.

01 / The system

One exchange, people and bots

02 / What each part does

Small responsibilities, connected behavior

API

Authenticate clients. Accept orders. Return results.

Matching

Maintain the book. Match compatible bids and asks.

Accounts

Track cash, holdings, and the effects of trades.

Market data

Expose liquidity and prices formed by trades.

Competition

Rank period performance. Accounts never reset; scoring remains open.

Bots

Provide market activity under similar rules, with effectively unlimited money.
