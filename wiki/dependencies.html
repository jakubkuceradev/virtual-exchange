Keep one trading lane

An accepted order must leave orders, fills, balances and reservations consistent. Matching, settlement and recovery share that guarantee; separate folders do not make separate deliverables.

Agree contracts, then split

Access and data delivery can run alongside core work once inputs, errors, identity, retry semantics and committed observations have concrete examples.

Build consumers against fixtures

Competition calculations, frontend views and bots can start with agreed examples. Fixtures must include absent prices and missing history. Live acceptance still requires their upstream capabilities.
